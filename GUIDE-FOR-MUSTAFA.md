# Everyday Plate — how to post 👨‍🍳

Everything happens in your **Kitchen** (the admin panel):

**👉 your-website.com/studio**

Sign in with the account you were invited with. It works on a phone too, so add it to your home screen.

There's also a small **"Kitchen login"** link at the bottom of every page.

---

## The menu on the left

| Menu | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Recipes** | Your recipes |
| **Videos & Shorts** | Your YouTube Shorts and videos |
| **Stories (blog)** | Longer posts and kitchen stories |
| **Featured on home page** | Recipes you've marked as featured |
| **Categories** | e.g. "Cypriot Classics", "Meze", "Desserts" |
| **Site settings** | Home page text, your photo, About page, social links |

---

## ➕ Add a recipe

1. Click **Recipes**, then the **✏️ pencil / +** button at the top.
2. Tab **1. Basics**: name, photo, short summary, categories, times, serves, difficulty.
   - Under **Web address**, click **Generate**.
   - After uploading a photo, you can drag the circle onto the most important part of the dish. The site will always keep that part in view.
3. Tab **2. Ingredients & method**
   - **Ingredients:** one per line (press Enter for the next one). For a small heading like "For the sauce", put the cursor on that line and choose **Heading** in the text style menu.
   - **Method:** one paragraph per step (press Enter for the next step). The website numbers the steps for you.
4. Tab **3. Extras** (optional): a YouTube link, the story behind the dish, and **Feature on the home page**.
5. Click the green **Publish** button at the bottom right. ✅

It's on the website within a minute.

## 🎬 Add a YouTube Short or video

1. On YouTube, press **Share → Copy link**.
2. Go to **Videos & Shorts → +** and paste the link. A preview appears straight away.
3. Add a title, click **Generate** for the web address, and choose **Short** or **Regular video**.
4. Optional: pick the **Linked recipe** so viewers can jump straight to it.
5. **Publish.** The thumbnail comes from YouTube automatically.

## ✍️ Write a story

**Stories (blog) → +**. Add a title, cover photo and summary, then write. Use the toolbar for headings and bold text, and the **+** button to insert photos or a YouTube video between paragraphs. **Publish.**

## 🏷️ Categories

Add as many as you like in **Categories**. Give each one a photo so it looks nice on the home page. Use **Position** (1, 2, 3…) to choose the order.

## ✨ Formatting your text

Every text box (summaries, descriptions, ingredients, method, tips, stories, About) has a small toolbar:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **B** | **Bold** |
| *I* | *Italic* |
| U | Underline |
| 🔗 | Turn the selected words into a link |
| Text style menu (says "Normal text") | **Centred text**, **Right-aligned text**, headings and quotes |

Select words and click a button, or use the keyboard: **Ctrl+B** for bold, **Ctrl+I** for italic and **Ctrl+U** for underline (**⌘** on a Mac). Alignment applies to the whole paragraph where your cursor is.

Titles and names stay plain. They're also used for the web address, the browser tab and Google results, and the website styles them for you.

## Good to know

- **Nothing is live until you press Publish.** Your work is saved automatically as a draft while you type.
- Made a mistake? Open the document's **History** (the clock icon at the top) to see or restore an earlier version.
- To **edit** something, open it, change it, and **Publish** again.
- The small **arrow next to Publish** opens more actions:
  - **View on website** opens the published page.
  - **Unpublish** removes it from the website but keeps it in your Kitchen.
  - **Delete** removes it completely.
- The home page only ever shows your **newest** posts, so it stays tidy as you add more. Everything else is under Recipes, Videos and Stories.
- Photos: use landscape photos for recipes and stories where you can. Any size is fine, because the site resizes them.
